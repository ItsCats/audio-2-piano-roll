Notices for the parts of this directory ported from OpenCV (https://github.com/opencv/opencv)
============================================================================================

geom.ts, cv.ts and subdiv2d.ts re-implement, in TypeScript, functions of OpenCV 4.x's imgproc module
that homr calls through opencv-python (the same algorithms and arithmetic, so the results match):

  subdivision2d.cpp (Subdiv2D), contours.cpp (findContours), convhull.cpp (convexHull),
  rotcalipers.cpp (minAreaRect), shapedescr.cpp (fitEllipse, contourArea, boundingRect),
  intersection.cpp (rotatedRectangleIntersection), geometry.cpp, drawing.cpp (fillPoly,
  fillConvexPoly, ellipse2Poly), clahe.cpp (CLAHE), resize.cpp (INTER_LINEAR), morph.dispatch.cpp /
  morph.simd.hpp (erode, dilate, getStructuringElement), box_filter.dispatch.cpp (blur), thresh.cpp
  (threshold), deriv.cpp (Laplacian), imgwarp.cpp (warpAffine, boxPoints), color.cpp /
  color_rgb.simd.hpp (BGR to gray).
